Goal

Picks maximize expected points: take every favorite, give the most points to the surest winners. Market lines are de-vigged moneylines; FPI is ESPN's model. Lock a pick you've already submitted, flip a game to take the underdog, or tune any probability — everything re-optimizes instantly.

Win the week plays against your pool instead of the odds. It simulates thousands of weeks with a field of opponents who mostly follow the lines, then finds the picks that most often finish first — usually by backing a well-chosen underdog with real points, so you gain when everyone else loses. It trades a few expected points for a better shot at the weekly prize; for season-long totals, stick with max points.
